# Test results: 12 synthetic cases

**All data is made up**: names, PAN/SSN/EIN/TAN, amounts and documents. The cases are defined in [`../mock/cases.js`](../mock/cases.js), and the rules they are judged against are in [`../mock/rules.js`](../mock/rules.js).

**How the test works.** The LLM stages (extraction, cause proposal, draft) are hand-written fixtures that represent what a model *would plausibly output*, including plausible mistakes (IN-05, US-05). The deterministic stages (validation, reproduction, rules engine, evidence-ledger guard, citation and amount trace) **run live** on those fixtures, in the browser and in Node. "Truth" is my hand label. **This measures whether the scaffolding catches or passes the right things. It does not measure model extraction accuracy on real documents.**

**Summary:** 10 / 12 handled correctly (2 closed automatically, 3 closed with reviewer flags, 5 correctly stopped or escalated), 2 wrong in v1. The evidence-ledger guard fixes US-05 and changes no other case. US-04 still needs a new rule.

## What the failures teach

1. **US-05: the residue gate checks arithmetic, not meaning.** A real line with the right amount can be cited as evidence for the wrong income. This is the design's weakest point. The ledger guard (each source line can explain one amount only) is a cheap, deterministic fix, but it only covers *reuse*. A model could still cite a line that coincidentally matches and isn't used anywhere else.
2. **US-04: a closed taxonomy is only as good as its list.** "Genuinely omitted" was treated as the end of the analysis. A practitioner would immediately ask about related expenses. The rule for business income should require that question.
3. **Not tested at all:** extraction from real CPC PDFs and broker statements, redaction quality on OCR text, and how often real notices fall outside the 6-label taxonomies. See brief §8.

## Re-running the deterministic checks

The engine in `mock/index.html` is plain JS. Opening the page and clicking through the cases re-computes everything. Toggle **Stop reusing evidence** (top right) to compare v1 and v2 on US-05.
